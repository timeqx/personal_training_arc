# Progress Scorecard

Update weekly. Score evidence, not confidence.

Scale:
- 0 = unknown
- 1 = recognize terminology
- 2 = can explain with notes
- 3 = can explain without notes and solve basic exercises
- 4 = can build/debug independently
- 5 = can design, review, teach, and defend tradeoffs

A 5 should be rare.
